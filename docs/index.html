Five Skies

A free iPhone app for daily prayer and the Quran. No ads, no accounts, no tracking.

What it does

  • Prayer times for your location, with a sky that follows the five daily prayers
  • Qibla direction
  • The Quran in Arabic, with bookmarks and search
  • Prayer tracker, Tasbih counter, Zakat estimate and Hijri calendar

Status

Five Skies is in development and is not yet on the App Store.

Sources

Quran text: Tanzil Project, Uthmani text, shown exactly as published. Prayer times and Qibla: the Adhan library by Batoul Apps.
