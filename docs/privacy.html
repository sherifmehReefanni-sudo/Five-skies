Privacy Policy

Effective date: [EFFECTIVE DATE]

Five Skies is built to know as little about you as possible. This page says plainly what the app does with information.

The short version

  • There are no accounts and no sign-in.
  • There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
  • We do not collect, sell or share personal information.

Location

If you allow it, the app uses your location to calculate prayer times and the Qibla direction. The calculation happens on your iPhone. Your location is not sent to us or to anyone else. You can turn location off in iPhone Settings at any time; the app then shows times for a labelled default city until you set a location.

What is stored on your iPhone

Your settings, the prayers you mark, your bookmarks, your last-read position and your Tasbih count are saved on your device only. Deleting the app deletes them.

Notifications

Prayer alerts are scheduled on your iPhone by the app itself. They are not sent from a server.

Internet access

The current version does not need the internet and does not contact any server. Later versions will let you load translations and recitations from Quran Foundation. When that is added, your iPhone will request that content over the internet, and like any internet request it will reveal your IP address to the servers that answer it. This policy will be updated before that feature is released.

Payments

The app is free. If optional tips are added, they will be handled by Apple through the App Store. We will not receive your payment details.

Children

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
