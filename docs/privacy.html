Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 27, 2026

This policy covers the Five Skies iPhone app. Five Skies is built to know as little about you as possible. This page says plainly what the app does with information, and who else is involved when it uses the internet.

The short version

  • There are no accounts and no sign-in.
  • There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
  • We do not collect, sell or share personal information. We have no server that stores anything about you.
  • A few features use the internet. Each one is listed below, with who answers the request.

Location

If you allow it, the app uses your location on your iPhone to calculate prayer times and the Qibla direction, and to show the name of the nearest city from a list built into the app. None of that leaves your iPhone.

When you search for mosques or halal food, the app asks Apple Maps for places near you, so Apple receives your approximate location for that search. Apple's own privacy policy applies to it. Your location is never sent to us.

You can turn location off in iPhone Settings at any time. The app then shows times for a labelled default city until you set a location.

Quran translations and recitations

Five Skies uses the Quran Foundation APIs for translations, transliteration and recitations.

  • Translations and transliteration are requested through our own relay, which runs on Cloudflare. The relay passes your request to Quran Foundation and passes the answer back. It stores no content and does not log what you request. To block abuse it counts requests per IP address for one minute and keeps nothing after that. Cloudflare, like any web host, handles your IP address to deliver the answer. Quran Foundation receives the relay's request, not your IP address.
  • Recitations and single-word audio play straight from Quran Foundation's audio servers, so those servers see your IP address while you listen.
  • Translations you choose are kept on your iPhone so they work offline, and are refreshed from Quran Foundation at least every 7 days when you are online.

What is stored on your iPhone

Your settings, the prayers you mark, pauses, goals, fasting log, dhikr counts, bookmarks, memorization progress, your last-read position and an optional first name for the greeting are saved on your device. Deleting the app deletes them.

iCloud backup (optional)

If you tap "Back up now", the app saves a copy of your tracker, goals, settings and progress to your own iCloud account, using Apple's iCloud key-value storage. Only you and Apple can reach it; we cannot see it. Your first name is not included. You can restore it on a new iPhone signed in to the same Apple account.

Notifications and alarms

Prayer alerts, reminders and the night alarm are scheduled on your iPhone by the app itself. They are not sent from a server.

Feedback

The feedback buttons open your own mail app with a message addressed to fiveskiesapp@gmail.com. Nothing is sent unless you send it. If you do, we receive your email address and what you write, and use them only to reply.

Payments

The app is free. Optional tips are handled by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your payment details.

Children

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
